I prefer work that is nerdy and fun to work that is canonical and dogmatically serious, and I choose my media according to the material conditions of each project. While I have most history with writing and textile work, in recent years this has included working with 3D software, game engines, electronics, airbrush, metal and inflatables, the latter as assistant at the polymer joining technology workshop at the University of Applied Arts Vienna.

Public space has its own rules for how we may act in it. As the duo public affection, Franz Siebler and I develop concepts and art in architecture that critically reflect on these rules, in physical as well as digital space.
