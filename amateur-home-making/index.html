zine series, poster, sticker-based decoration game · A5/A3 print · with Franz Siebler, as public affection · 2026

What does home become when it's something to be rated, upgraded and shown? Growing out of research with Franz Siebler for public affection, amateur home making is a pocket-sized study of how digital tools reshape domestic life, whether by inspiring renovations or by asking us to perform domesticity for an audience. Four separate but combinable publications each take on one phenomenon: home-decorating simulator games, the Reddit community r/malelivingspace, room-makeover redemption content and Zoom backgrounds. Together they ask which tools people turn to when they want to take control of their surroundings, and what kind of discourse this produces. Unfolded and combined, the series becomes a poster and a sticker-based decoration game.
