I am a visual artist and cross-disciplinary researcher with a background in philosophy.

My work starts from the observation that emerging digital technologies define new rules and constraints for our agency, and in doing so, offer us new social, political and epistemic games to play. In my research, I am primarily interested in how this affects what we believe: how new games redefine how we discuss and inform ourselves and what seems to become rational once we start playing them.

In my artistic practice, I focus on the material and visceral side of these games, confronting how it feels to play. My work revolves around the sensation of technologically mediated interaction, recreation, yearning, speculation, and connection. A recurring focus is what I call technologies of artificial entertainment: slot games, chatbots, animatronics and fun-fair attractions - narrow machines designed to isolate and mimic particular human emotions. Alongside playable experiences, my observations find form in costume and performance, participatory installations, sculpture and printed matter. I currently work as an assistant in the workshop for polymer joining technology at the University of Applied Arts Vienna.

I am based between Vienna and Berlin.

As an artist, I have worked with, among others: SpielArt Theaterfestival, Villa Stuck, Potsdamer Tanztage, Live from Earth Klub, Zentrale Zentrale, White Noise Seoul, Symposium Urbanum Nürnberg and Donaufestival. As a researcher, I have taught or worked at the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy, the Munich Center for Ethics, Freie Universität Berlin, the Alfred Toepfer Stiftung's Europakolleg Cultural Sustainability, Forum für Freiraum and the German Academic Scholarship Foundation.

I co-founded and edited the satirical philosophy magazine Die Funzel. Halbjahresschrift für prätentiöse Studien., and have written for Arts of the Working Class, springerin and Süddeutsche Zeitung.
