I am a visual artist and cross-disciplinary researcher with a background in philosophy.

My work starts from the observation that emerging digital technologies define new rules and constraints for our agency, and in doing so, offer us new social, political and epistemic games to play. In my research, I am interested in the weird, funny and harmful beliefs and actions that seem to become rational once we start playing them.

In my artistic practice, I focus on the material and visceral side of these games, confronting how it feels to play. My work recreates the affective sensations of digital interaction, such as the coexistence of surveillance and recreation, hope as a yearning form of probabilistic speculation, or connectedness in physical absence. A recurring focus is what I call technologies of artificial entertainment: slot games, chatbots, animatronics and fun-fair attractions - machines designed to make us obsessed with an abstract and very narrow kind of ecstasy. Alongside playable experiences, my observations find form in costume and performance, participatory installations, sculpture and printed matter.

As an artist, I have worked with, among others, SpielArt Theaterfestival, Villa Stuck, Potsdamer Tanztage, Live from Earth Klub, Zentrale Zentrale, White Noise Seoul, Symposium Urbanum Nürnberg and Donaufestival. As a researcher, I have taught or worked at the Munich Center for Mathematical Philosophy, the Munich Center for Ethics, Freie Universität Berlin, the Alfred Toepfer Stiftung's Europakolleg Cultural Sustainability, Forum für Freiraum and the German Academic Scholarship Foundation. I co-founded and edited the satirical philosophy magazine Die Funzel, and have written for Arts of the Working Class, springerin and Süddeutsche Zeitung.

I am based between Vienna and Berlin.
